# Logo provenance

![GryphDash logo](logo.svg)

The GryphDash logo is based on [Purple Cyber Griffin](https://openclipart.org/detail/314279/purple-cyber-griffin), an artwork by **Falco276**.

The SVG was downloaded from Openclipart, its Inkscape and licensing metadata was removed so the repository contains only the artwork, and the artwork was mirrored horizontally within its original `771 × 805` viewBox. The resulting file is served locally as `logo.svg` and embedded in the dashboard.

Openclipart states that its graphics are released under [CC0/public domain](https://openclipart.org/faq). This permits copying, modification, redistribution, and commercial use without asking permission or providing attribution. GryphDash credits Falco276 as a courtesy.
